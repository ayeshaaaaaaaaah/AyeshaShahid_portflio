FlyRank AI Clustering Engine

Segmenting 30,000 mixed performance rows into 4 marketing-actionable archetypes, without vanity-metric bias.

Jump to pipeline →
Context & problem framing

The ask was vague

FlyRank's content team had a 30K-row dataset tracking reach, watch time, engagement rate, and content tags — but no defined strategy for what to prioritize. A standard dashboard would show which numbers were high or low, but not why content performed the way it did, or how to turn that into a repeatable strategy.

Engineering execution & pipeline architecture

From raw rows to validated clusters

Preprocessing is encapsulated in a scikit-learn ColumnTransformer so every transformation is fit strictly inside training folds — no global feature leakage. Continuous engagement ratios go through StandardScaler; text metadata tags go through TfidfVectorizer (max_features=100). The two are concatenated into one composite matrix before clustering.

Archetype deliverables & business impact

4 archetypes, each with a strategic action

Core Retention Engine

Completion rate > 65% · Conversion rate > 4% · Reach < 10K

Increase paid distribution — content retains users but lacks top-of-funnel reach.

High-Volume Conversion

Reach > 50K · Conversion rate > 3.5% · Completion rate > 40%

Scale production — benchmark this format across other channels.

Viral Vanity Spikes

Reach > 100K · Completion rate < 15% · Conversion rate < 0.5%

Rework hook quality — high reach fails to convert or retain.

Underperforming / Deprecation

Reach < 5K · Completion rate < 10% · Conversion rate < 0.1%

Deprecate this format — low engagement across the funnel.
